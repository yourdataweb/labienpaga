The restaurant

A proper eating house on the square

La Bien Pagá is a market-kitchen restaurant on Plaça Soler i Carbonell, in the centre of Vilanova. The set lunch is the heart of it: written each morning from what the market has.

The cooking is direct and unfussy: fresh produce, classic preparations and portions built for sharing. The midday menu — starter, main, dessert and a drink — is what brings the neighbourhood in every day.

The dining room is warm and family-run, and in summer the tables spill onto the square. It works for a work lunch as well as a long family afternoon.
